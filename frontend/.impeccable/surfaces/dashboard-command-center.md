# Dashboard command center

## Scope
Operate surfaces for admin operations and member learning, including the portal chooser and authenticated shells.

## Direction contract

### THESIS
The portal is a mission-control workspace: every screen answers what needs attention, what happens next, and where to act. It refuses decorative glass panels and separates operational urgency from learning momentum.

### OWN-WORLD
A restrained blue-black command surface uses cool paper backgrounds, crisp 1px rules, compact rectangular controls, tabular numerals, and a warm gold signal for selected or urgent states. Admin navigation is dense and grouped; member navigation is calm and focused.

### STORY
Admin sees the operating pulse, today's agenda, and queues that need intervention before deeper management links. Member sees the next lesson or session, progress, and a clear route back into learning without operational clutter.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
Desktop admin opens with a compact utility bar, greeting, live date, four operational measures, then a two-column control board: today's sessions and action queue. Mobile collapses to one column with the same order and a bottom-safe nav. Member opens with greeting, child/course context, next action, progress, and support.

### FORM
