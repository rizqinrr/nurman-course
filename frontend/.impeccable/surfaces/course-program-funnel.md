# Surface brief — /course/program funnel

## Scope
Persuade: parents choosing a tutoring path from `/course/program` through material, level, and schedule configuration. Success is selecting a route quickly and reaching WhatsApp without losing context.

## Audience and job
A parent needs to identify the right learning route in seconds, compare only the minimum useful detail, and continue with confidence. Preserve all existing copy, data, routes, pricing, and WhatsApp behavior.

## Direction contract

### THESIS
Turn the funnel into a branching learning map: one starting point, three clear routes, and progressively narrower choices. Refuses the generic centered card stack and makes the next action obvious on every screen.

### OWN-WORLD
Extend the landing's Peta Belajar language: light blue map ground, deep ink navy, Nurman blue for active route, warm yellow for the destination action, thin plotted lines, route nodes, and compact worksheet-like panels. Use the existing landing typography and tokens; no new visual identity.

### STORY
The visitor starts at a shared point, chooses Materi, Jenjang, or Calistung, then follows a visible route from category to level to schedule. Each page answers “where am I?”, “what do I choose now?”, and “what happens next?” without hiding the CTA.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
Mobile-first: a compact top rail with back and route progress, a strong page title, then a branching route diagram that feeds three large selectable nodes. On list/detail pages, show the primary choices before secondary explanation; on configuration, keep the sticky WhatsApp action visible with the estimated price.

### FORM
Peta bercabang, selected by the user; seed key 5ed43c21. Route nodes and connecting lines are functional structure, not decoration. Responsive behavior stacks branches on mobile and opens into a two-column map/list composition at larger widths.

### FINISH
unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Unresolved
No new claims, assets, payment behavior, backend calls, or route changes. Browser verification must cover 390px and desktop widths.
